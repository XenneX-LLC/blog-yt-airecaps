There is more good video being made right now than at any point in history. Long-form interviews, deep technical breakdowns, thoughtful explainers, weekly shows from creators you actually trust. The problem isn't finding things worth watching. It's that there's far more worth watching than anyone has hours in a week.

AI Recaps exists for that gap.

## The Problem We're Actually Solving

Most people who follow more than a handful of channels have landed in the same place: a subscription feed they can't keep up with and a Watch Later list that only grows. The usual coping strategies don't really work. Watching at 2x still takes hours. Skimming thumbnails means guessing. Skipping everything means missing the one video that actually mattered this week.

The real cost isn't the time spent watching. It's the time spent deciding, and the good content that gets lost because deciding took too long.

## What AI Recaps Does

AI Recaps turns long videos into clear, readable summaries: what the video covers, the main points, and the conclusions, laid out so you can take it in within a couple of minutes instead of an hour.

That does two jobs at once.

**It helps you decide.** A good summary tells you quickly whether a video deserves your full attention. The ones that do, you watch properly. The ones that don't, you skip without the nagging feeling you might have missed something.

**It gets you the value from the rest.** For a lot of content, the summary is the value. The key ideas from an interview, the takeaways from an update, the one section of a long video you actually needed. You stay informed across far more than you could ever watch.

## What We Don't Pretend

A summary isn't a replacement for everything. We've written a whole series about where summaries fall short: comedy loses its timing, true crime loses its reveal, reality TV loses the reactions that make it fun. Some content deserves to be watched start to finish, and we'd rather tell you that honestly than claim a summary works for every video.

If you're wondering how this differs from pasting a link into a general-purpose chatbot, we've [written about that directly](/blog/why-ai-recaps-exists-when-chatgpt-does-this). The short version: a one-off summary answers "what's in this video?" AI Recaps is built around the bigger question of staying on top of everything you follow.

## Who It's For

AI Recaps is for anyone whose interest in video has outgrown the time they have to watch it. People who follow creators for real information, not just background noise. People who want to stay current in their field, their hobbies, or the topics they care about without giving up their evenings to do it.

## The Bottom Line

The best video on the internet shouldn't be lost just because there's too much of it. AI Recaps helps you find what's worth watching, get the value from what isn't, and stay caught up on everything you follow without needing more hours in the day.
